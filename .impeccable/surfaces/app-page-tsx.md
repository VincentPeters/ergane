# Surface: app/page.tsx (the generator screen)

Mode: Operate. Audience: hobbyists at home with their own laser. Job: pick grid, material and fit, shape a part, check it, export a cut file; first run calibrates kerf with the coupon.
Constraints: preview and export come from the same path list; export colours stay pure #FF0000 / #00FF00 / #0000FF; validation blocks export and names the rule. Kit drawer and sheet layout are later milestones and are not faked on screen.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The kit is charted like a sky. The generator screen is a star-atlas plate: holes are magnitude dots, the outline is the line joining them, named for Athena Ergane (patron of craftspeople). It refuses the grey CAD inspector with a white canvas.

OWN-WORLD: Chart navy ground across shell and work area, chalk white for parts and text, red-flashlight amber for annotations, selection and focus, muted blue-grey coordinate gridlines, red only for failed rules. Spectral light italic names sections; Public Sans with tabular figures carries every value. Hairline borders, 6 px radii, no shadows.

STORY: The maker sees their part measured like a charted object, trusts the numbers, calibrates once with the coupon, and exports.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 52 px top bar (two-hole strip mark, name, Grid, Material, Kerf, Fit, Calibrate link, chalk Export button). Left rail 228 px: element library by group. Centre: chart plate filling the remaining space, part plotted with mm rulers, pitch grid through hole centres, amber dimension annotations, dashed amber selection ring, Laser colours switch and scale readout. Right column 304 px: Parameters, Measured (tabular), rule status.

FORM: challenger fused (notation-diagram-systems-star-atlas), chosen over assigned candidate 3 of 7. seed:e5fc2806eac8a1ff

Signature interaction: the preview is a chart plate; holes plotted as magnitude dots sized by hole type, Laser colours switch recolours the same plot into cut layers.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
